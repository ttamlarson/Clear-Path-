Your data

Your budget is stored on this device in this browser. It is not automatically synced to another device or uploaded to a bank.

Export an encrypted backup before clearing browser data or changing devices.

Privacy: PDF statements are parsed in this browser. ClearPath does not upload the PDF or transaction data. The PDF parsing library is loaded from a public CDN when you first use PDF import; that library request does not contain your statement.

ClearPath stores data locally on this device. It does not connect to your bank or provide financial advice. Keep an exported backup somewhere safe.
